# Geology (dual enrollment)

Honors geology for 12th graders, taught at college level through dual enrollment. One teacher (Renick).

## Course facts

- Audience: college-level 12th graders. Pitch content, vocabulary and quantitative work at an introductory college geology level, not a standard high school level.
- Grading: percentage scale (0-100%). Do not use the 4-point learning-target scale from the Earth Science course.
