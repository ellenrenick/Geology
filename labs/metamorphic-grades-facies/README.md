# Lab 7.5 Metamorphic Grades and Facies

Self-contained guided lab site (`index.html`). Students work through Part A (Barrow zones), Part B (Al2SiO5 polymorphs on a P-T diagram), and Part C (Eskola facies and the subduction zone). Answers save in the browser and can be copied into Canvas.

Handout: none yet (source is the textbook Activity 7.5 PDF).

## Student flow
- Site: https://claude.ai/artifact/SqiBUYjArpzEdr2FBn5i1p

- Students copy their answers from the bottom of the page and paste them into the Canvas text entry assignment.
