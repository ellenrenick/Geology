Tests: foliation (rotate under a light) · acid (one drop dilute HCl, goggles on) · hardness: fingernail 2.5, copper coin about 3.5, steel nail and glass about 5.5, quartz 7.
Grade sequence: slate, phyllite, schist, gneiss with rising T and P. Phyllite (fine, satiny sheen, wavy surfaces) is not in this collection. Name schists by prominent minerals (garnet, hornblende, etc.).
