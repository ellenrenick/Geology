Tests used in the key

Foliation

Rotate the specimen under a light. Foliated rocks have flaky minerals lined up or light and dark mineral bands, so they look streaked or split in one direction. Nonfoliated rocks are massive, with equal-sized crystals that interlock.

Acid test

Use one drop of dilute (about 5 to 10%) HCl on a fresh surface, with goggles on. Calcite marble fizzes at once. Dolomitic marble reacts weakly until you scratch a little powder from the surface.

Hardness (Mohs)

Fingernail 2.5, copper coin about 3.5, steel nail and glass about 5.5, quartz 7. Try to scratch the glass plate with the specimen. If it leaves a scratch on the glass, the specimen is harder than 5.5.
